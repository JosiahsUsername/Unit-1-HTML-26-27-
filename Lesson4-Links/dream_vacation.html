Trip Research

Although I have been to Korea a year ago, i would love to again, with cheaper things and just to have a more relaxed vacation instead of rshing all the activities. VisitKorea is a great website for anyone needing to plan for a trip to South Korea like I am. Of course the start of a trip is definetly the tourism guide, but even with that you might not know where to go. That's where TripAdvisor comes in handy. You can find a couple great picks as well as must dos for your trip to South Korea. Lastly, it gets awfully hot, so you need a weather website! KMAwould be my choice for their accurate weather. After that, you're basically all set planning wise.

Getting There & Staying There

Flight & Hotel Booking

To help you plan flights, I would suggest 2 websites. Expedia and Booking.com. Both will help you for things like flights, and even finding hotels!

Transportation Info

To help you out with finding transportation, I have 2 webistes for you. The first is good old Google Maps! (Make sure to search up south Korea and find places based on your location). The second is to help you with public transit, it's called Trip.com.
