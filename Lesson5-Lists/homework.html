Grilled Cheese

Grilled Cheese is a simple dish but a delicious one at that. If you just want something nice and quick, this is definetly the thing to do. If you were to have tomato soup with it, its even better, but right now we will focus on the main dish itself.

  • Ingredients
  • Steps
  • Notes

Ingredients

  • Bread
  • Butter
  • Mozzarella cheese
  • 2 Chedar cheese
  • Tomato soup (optional)
  • Bacon (optional)

Steps

  1. First we have to prep the pan by putting some butter on it. It'll make the bread taste nicer and it can get a nice crispy bottom and top if made correctly.
  2. Then we will put 3 pieces of cheese in between 2 pieces of bread, I perfer to have more chedar than mozzarella, so that's what we will be doing. Put it in an alternating pattern, it'll end up being bread, chedar, mozzarella, chedar, and then bread again. (Note that you don't have to get those exact amounts of cheese, 1 of any type is enough)
  3. We will continue by adding anything to make the grilled cheese better, for example, I think putting bacon in between the cheese makes it taste better. You don't have to do that though.
  4. Put the sandwhich on the pan, put it over low heat and keep it there till it has a golden brown crust.
  5. Flip to the other side and repeat.
  6. Present your dish with tomato soup (optional). You should also have nice melted cheese after cooking.

Notes

  • Time will definetly vary for getting the golden crust. It might be a good idea to keep it there for longer if the cheese didn't fully melt.
    • There was one time I was making this, and one of the grilled cheeses took over 20 minutes to get a good crust, even after I upped th heat! The cheese was amazingly melted but the bread was not golden brown. So it's fine for it to be imperfect.
  • Of course, experimenting with a simple ish like this is easy and fun! I personally think bacon would be great to put here, but I would suggest cooking it a bit before cooking. Other examples could include:
    • Ham
    • Beef
    • Spam (preferably thinly cut)
    • Cucumbers
    • And more! Feel free to experiment!
